Our Story

A neighborhood-focused lawn-care company

Lawn Trim is a small, local crew led by Felix. The company says Felix has spent three decades behind a mower and three years running paid jobs before starting Lawn Trim with a goal of providing dependable work at a price customers can keep.

The business is built around repeat customers rather than one-time upsells. That means the relationship continues after the first visit, with service designed around recurring lawn maintenance and practical property needs.

Lawn Trim says its work is focused on Northeast Houston and nearby service routes, with neighborhood pride at the center of how it approaches each property.

How Pricing Works

Clear pricing before service

Lawn Trim describes its pricing as transparent and based on lawn size, with weekly, biweekly and monthly recurring plans available. If grass is heavily overgrown, a small time-and-wear fee may apply, and the company says that charge is shown before booking.

Debris haul-away is available on request. The goal is to make the scope understandable before service starts rather than introducing unexpected add-ons later.

  • Flat pricing based on lawn size.
  • Optional weekly, biweekly or monthly plans.
  • Long-grass time-and-wear charge disclosed before booking.
  • Debris haul-away available on request.
