What Is Pressure Washing?

Pressure washing uses pressurized water to clean suitable exterior surfaces. It is commonly considered for hard outdoor areas where ordinary rinsing does not remove built-up dirt or grime effectively.

The correct cleaning approach depends on the material, condition and type of buildup. Before work starts, the service scope should be clear so the homeowner understands what surfaces are included.

How Often Should You Pressure Wash?

There is no single schedule that fits every property. Frequency depends on surface type, exposure, amount of buildup, surrounding vegetation and the homeowner's preferred appearance.

Some properties may benefit from occasional deep cleaning, while high-use or highly visible surfaces may need attention more often.

Pressure Washing vs. Regular Rinsing

Regular hose rinsing can remove loose dirt, but deeper surface buildup may need more cleaning power. Pressure washing is better suited to appropriate surfaces where more thorough cleaning is needed.

For delicate or coated surfaces, the cleaning approach should be evaluated carefully to avoid unnecessary wear or damage.
