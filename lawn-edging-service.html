What Is Lawn Edging?

Lawn edging creates a visible boundary between your grass and another surface or landscape area. Proper edging can give sidewalks, driveways, flower beds and walkways a cleaner appearance.

Lawn Edging Services

Sidewalk Edging

Create a clear line between your lawn and sidewalk for a cleaner walkway appearance.

Driveway Edging

Keep grass from spreading over driveway edges and help give the entrance to your property a cleaner look.

Flower Bed Edging

Define landscape beds and keep the transition between grass and planting areas neat.

Walkway Edging

Maintain crisp lawn borders around pathways and frequently used outdoor walkways.

Curb Edging

Keep the grass line along street curbs looking organized and maintained.

Property Border Edging

Create cleaner transitions around other lawn and landscape areas throughout the property.

Why Lawn Edging Matters

A lawn can be freshly mowed and still look unfinished if the grass has grown over surrounding hard surfaces. Edging adds the final definition to the lawn.

  • Creates clean and visible lawn borders
  • Improves the appearance of sidewalks and driveways
  • Helps landscape beds look more defined
  • Complements lawn mowing and trimming
  • Supports a more finished overall appearance
  • Helps the property look regularly maintained

Lawn Edging and Lawn Mowing

Lawn edging and mowing work well together. Mowing controls grass height while edging defines the boundary between turf and surrounding surfaces.

Lawn Trim LLC lists lawn mowing and edging together as a core service and describes the result as clean cut lines and even height.
