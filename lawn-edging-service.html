What Is Lawn Edging?

Lawn edging is the process of creating a clean, defined line between your grass and another surface or landscape area. It helps prevent grass from growing into sidewalks, driveways, flower beds, patios, and other parts of your property.

A sharp lawn edge can make the entire yard appear more organized. It also gives mowing and lawn maintenance work a more finished appearance.

Lawn Edging vs String Trimming

String trimming and edging are related but different lawn maintenance tasks. String trimming helps cut grass around obstacles, fences, trees, posts, and areas that a mower cannot easily reach.

Edging focuses on creating a defined border between turf and another surface, such as a sidewalk or driveway. Using both services together can produce a more complete lawn finish.
